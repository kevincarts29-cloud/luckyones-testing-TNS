Privacy Policy

Written to be read. If anything here is unclear, ask us.

1. The short version

We collect the minimum we need to run our products and answer you. We do not sell personal data, we do not run advertising networks on this site, and we will tell you before we change anything material on this page.

2. What we collect

If you create an account: your name, work email, company, and anything else your workspace needs to function. We also store billing details through our payment processor; full card numbers never touch our servers.

If you use our products: the data those products are meant to handle. We process it only to deliver the service to you. We do not use your customer data to train models, and we will not start without telling you in writing if that ever changes.

If you use this website: standard technical data such as IP address, browser, and pages visited, kept only for security and to see which parts of the site are useful.

3. AI and your data

Our AI features send only the context needed for the task you triggered. Where a third-party model provider is involved, the request is sent under their API terms and is not used to train their models. We keep a log of requests so we can debug problems, and those logs are removed on a rolling 90-day basis.

4. Cookies

Three things: keeping you signed in, remembering your interface preferences, and counting visits. There are no advertising cookies on this site and no third-party trackers embedded in our pages. You can block cookies in your browser, though you will be signed out each time you visit.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send it somewhere else. We action these within 30 days and do not charge for them.

If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your local data protection authority, and we would rather you told us first so we can fix it.

6. Sharing and processors

We share data only with the service providers that run our infrastructure, payments, email, and monitoring, each under a written agreement limiting what they can do with it. We will disclose data if legally required, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

7. Security and retention

Data in transit is encrypted, data at rest is encrypted, access is restricted on a need-to-know basis, and every administrative action is logged. We retain account data for as long as your account is active, then hold it for 30 days before deletion, apart from records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

No system is perfect. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant regulator without undue delay.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16, and if we discover that we have, we delete it.

9. Changes

If we make a material change we will email account holders at least 30 days beforehand and update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the service after that counts as acceptance.

10. Contact

privacy@technova-solutions.com. A person reads these, usually the same person who works on the product, which is unusual and deliberate.
