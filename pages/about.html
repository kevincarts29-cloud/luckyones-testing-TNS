About TechNova

A small team that has been building software for other people's teams since 2016.

We started in 2016 with two people, one client, and a strong opinion that most software projects fail because nobody defined the actual problem before anyone opened an editor. That opinion has cost us work, and it has saved us considerably more.

Ten years on we are still small, still hands-on, and still a bit suspicious of projects that need a 200-slide deck before anyone has seen the thing work. We build SaaS products and applied AI, we maintain what we build, and we say no more often than we say yes.

How we work

Three commitments that decide who we work well with.

Define it first

Before we quote anything we sit with the people doing the work and agree on what "fixed" looks like. Half the projects we walk away from fail this test in the first conversation.

Show work early

You see something running every week or two. It will be rough, and that is the point. Finding out it is wrong early costs nothing.

Leave you able to run it

Repository, infrastructure, and documentation are yours from day one. We would rather be useful than indispensable, and it turns out that works well for us too.
