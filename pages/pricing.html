Questions people ask first

Can I change plans later?

Yes, at any time. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades take effect next cycle. We prorate rather than making you pay twice.

Are there setup or onboarding fees?

Usually not. If your project involves a data migration, an unusual SSO setup, or a compliance review, we quote that as a one-off before any work starts.

Can I cancel?

Any time, with 30 days' notice on ongoing plans. We export your data in a standard format on request. No exit fees, no awkward calls.

How do you handle AI costs?

You get a monthly cap that is visible on the dashboard. If you approach it, we tell you before we spend it, not on the next invoice.

Who owns the code and data?

You do, in writing, from the start. Everything we build for you is yours, including the repository, the infrastructure, and the documentation.

Do you offer discounts?

Annual billing saves two months. Registered non-profits and early-stage startups can ask about reduced rates, and we say yes more often than not.

Not sure which plan fits?

Send us a paragraph about what you are trying to fix. We will suggest a plan, or tell you honestly that you do not need us yet.
