Ways to partner with us

Three arrangements, depending on how you would rather work.

Referral partner

You know a team with a problem we can solve. We work together on the introduction and stay in the loop, with a share of the first year.

  • 15% of first-year revenue
  • Help with the first conversation
  • Co-marketing when it makes sense
  • No minimum volume

Delivery partner

You deliver projects to your clients and need a specialist team for the build. We slot in behind the scenes, branded however you need.

  • White-label delivery
  • Shared project plan and milestones
  • Capacity without hiring
  • Named technical contact

Technology partner

You build a product we integrate with, or a tool our clients use. We would rather help you get into the hands of teams that will actually adopt you.

  • Direct integration support
  • Listing in our partner pages
  • Early access to our roadmap
  • Technical review of your API docs

What we ask of partners

Introduce us properly

A warm introduction beats a cold email every time. If we turn up to a call and the client has not heard of us, we are starting from nothing.

Be honest about scope

If a project is a bad fit, say so. We would rather lose the referral than spend four months on something that was never going to work.

Keep us in the loop

We cannot help when we are the last to hear something. A quick message is all it takes, usually.

Keep your own promises

We work with a handful of partners precisely so we can trust them. That is a small number partly because trust does not scale automatically.
