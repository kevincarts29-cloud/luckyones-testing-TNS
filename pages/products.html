Our products

Six things we have built and maintained for real teams. Each one started as a specific annoyance, not a market trend.

FlowDesk

Workflow Automation

Most teams do not have a people problem with their processes. They have a copy-paste problem. FlowDesk connects the tools you already pay for and moves the data between them, so nobody is retyping the same order into three systems at 6pm on a Friday.

  • No-code workflow builder with branching logic
  • Integrations with the common SaaS tools
  • Retries and alerts when a step fails
  • Full run history you can audit later
  • Scheduling, triggers, and manual override

See pricing

CopilotKit

Applied AI Assistants

An AI assistant scoped to a handful of tasks your team actually repeats: drafting, summarising, triaging, and first-pass research. It answers from your own documents and shows its sources, so people can check the answer instead of guessing whether to trust it.

  • Task-specific assistants rather than one vague chatbot
  • Grounded in your docs, with citations on every claim
  • A human review step before anything goes out
  • Evaluated against real tasks before it ships
  • Usage caps you control, with no surprise bills

See pricing

ClearView

Data & Reporting

Reporting that gets read. Connect your sources once and get a short weekly digest with the same handful of numbers everybody agrees on, formatted so someone can act on it in under a minute. If a number has two owners, it does not survive the week.

  • Agreed metric definitions, versioned
  • Scheduled email or Slack digests
  • Role-based visibility on sensitive data
  • Data quality checks with visible gaps
  • Clean export to your existing BI tool

See pricing

VaultID

Identity & Access

One login for everything, and access rules that match how your organisation really works rather than how the org chart claims it does. The part everyone underestimates is offboarding: this one actually revokes things, and you can prove it later.

  • Single sign-on via SAML and OIDC
  • Role and group-based access
  • Scheduled access reviews with reminders
  • Offboarding that removes access everywhere
  • Exportable audit trail for your auditors

See pricing

RelayDesk

Customer Support Hub

A shared inbox for support, sales, and onboarding questions. Everything about a customer lives in one thread, so nobody has to ask who already replied or hunt through someone else's drafts to find the answer they gave last week.

  • Unified team inbox with assignment
  • SLA timers and automatic escalation
  • Macros and saved replies
  • Internal notes that stay internal
  • Full customer history in one view

See pricing

Knowledge Base

Internal Documentation

Docs that stay current, because updating them is easy and searching them actually works. Ask a question in plain language and it answers from your content, then points you at the page so you can read the full thing yourself.

  • Clean editor with version history
  • Answers grounded in your own docs
  • Permissions that mirror your organisation
  • Search analytics showing knowledge gaps
  • Import from Notion, Confluence, or Markdown
